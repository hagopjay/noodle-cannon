NOODLE CANNON

Five ways to play one physics toy: a cannon, a limp crash-test noodle, six kinds of ammo and a box of toys. In the air you spin, tuck (spin faster, like a diver), spread out to glide, and puff. Different kinds of trick raise the multiplier. Play them all, then pick the one to grow.

CONCEPT A

Launch Party

Five shots into one seeded field of crate towers, TNT, bumpers, trampolines and balloons. Damage stays, so every shot rebuilds the playground for the next.

  • Physics fun: chain reactions, collapses, ragdoll piles
  • Return hook: daily field, ghost of your best shot
  • Feels like: Angry Birds × Happy Wheels
CONCEPT B

Barrel Relay

An endless chain of cannons over the sea. Each one catches you; swivelling, swinging and sliding cannons turn the fire button into timing. Two puffs per hop to save a bad shot.

  • Physics fun: air control, last-second puff saves, slow-mo catches
  • Return hook: one more run, rising difficulty
  • Feels like: DK barrel blasts × Flappy tension
CONCEPT C

Flop Golf

The cannon is your club, the noodle is your ball. It rolls to wherever you came to rest. Hills, water, sand, windmills and crate walls between you and the cup.

  • Physics fun: reading tumbles, bounces and slopes
  • Return hook: three daily holes, par, scorecard
  • Feels like: Golf With Your Friends × QWOP
CONCEPT D · NEW

Noodle Siege

Two forts, three crowned kings each, wind that changes every turn. Take turns firing every kind of ammo; knock all three enemy kings to the ground.

  • Physics fun: artillery arcs, wind, collapsing forts, cannonball riding
  • Return hook: rivalry, against the CPU or a friend on one screen
  • Feels like: Worms × Angry Birds
CONCEPT E · NEW

Toy Box

No rules. Unlimited shots, every ammo, and you drop the toys: fans, pendulums, seesaws, rope bridges, bowling pins, balloon crates, TNT forts.

  • Physics fun: build contraptions, then wreck them
  • Return hook: experimentation, shareable chaos
  • Feels like: Garry's Mod in 2D
Z toggles slow motion
Course:
Aim: mouse, or WS angle · AD power. Fire: click / Space. Ammo: 1–6 or Q. In the air: ←→ spin · hold ↓/Shift tuck · hold E glide · Space ammo action / puff (or click toward the pointer). Z slow motion. R retry · N new course · M sound · Esc menu. Touch: drag to aim, lift to fire, tap to puff.
